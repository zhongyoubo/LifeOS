# LifeOS v0.1.2 Candidate — Human Validation Pack

**English** | [简体中文](./v0.1.2-test-pack.zh-CN.md)

> Status: **HUMAN VALIDATION BASELINE**  
> Date: 2026-09-04

## Frozen Candidate Semantics

During Human Validation freeze: C1 = Learning Strategy Selection; C2 = Assistance Selection + Contextual Capability Evidence; C3 = Judgment Calibration + Reasoning Lens + Candidate Transfer Lesson; Kernel = Single Orchestrator + Stop Owner; Activation = Sparse; Evidence = Explicit Ownership; User Output = Problem-first / architecture-hidden. Do not expand protocols during testing except to remediate P0/P1 issues.

## Research Questions

Test whether C1 improves learning-strategy fit without framework overhead; C2 adapts assistance based on actual performance without reducing outcome quality; C3 improves evidence calibration for consequential judgments without analysis paralysis; combined candidates remain natural and low-cost; and users show behavioral autonomy/transfer rather than merely reporting that LifeOS felt helpful.

## Minimum Sample

Round 1: at least 5 real users, 15 real task/problem episodes, preferably 3 sessions per longitudinal C2 user, approximately 7-day follow-up for action/outcome cases, at least one repeated/similar task per longitudinal user, and at least one new-domain/boundary case across the cohort.

## Required Case Mix

Use real C1 learning tasks; longitudinal C2 interactions; naturally occurring consequential C3 judgments; mixed candidate problems; and Quick Controls such as factual, reversible or 15–20 minute learning requests. Do not manufacture risky decisions for testing.

## Session Record

Record Case ID, pseudonymous User ID, date, problem/goal, importance, Runtime Level, candidate activation, internal Assistance Mode, independent work before LifeOS, LifeOS contribution, the decision/next action actually accepted by the user, checkpoint, Process Cost, immediate clarity/confidence before and after, and risks/notes. Record the user's accepted action, not merely the AI recommendation.

## Evidence Ownership

C1 records Target Evidence: learning goal, target depth, success evidence defined before learning, first practice action and later observed evidence. C2 records contextual Capability Evidence: capability, task type, domain/context, observed behavior, assistance, independent portion, outcome/feedback, transfer/counterevidence, confidence and last observation. C3 records Judgment Evidence: initial judgment, material known/assumed distinction, critical unknown, plausible alternative, disconfirming evidence, reversibility/downside, updated judgment, change condition, action and later outcome.

A Candidate Transfer Lesson is not Observed Transfer Evidence. Only later independent behavior qualifies as transfer evidence.

## Follow-up

At roughly 7 days for action cases, record whether action was initiated/completed/advanced, what happened, whether judgment changed, what was learned, whether any structure was independently reused, whether the same amount of help would be needed next time, and any dependency/negative signal. Add 14–30 day follow-up for longer learning or capability cases when useful.

## Metrics

Primary: Clarity Delta, Action Initiation, Follow-through, Outcome Direction, Autonomy and Process Cost. C1: Strategy Fit, Success Evidence Quality, Learning Action Initiation. C2: Assistance Appropriateness, Independent Portion, Assistance Adaptation, Dependency Risk. C3: Calibration Quality, Decision Support, Action Sufficiency, Analysis Paralysis incidence. Cross-cutting: Transfer Evidence, Sparse Activation correctness and User-visible Framework Burden.

## Behavioral Autonomy

Autonomy is not reduced LifeOS usage. Observe independent problem framing, unknown identification, judgment formation/explanation, next-action choice, feedback updating, later structural recognition and appropriate help-seeking. Score 1–5 only for the specific capability/context, never as a global user level.

## Process Cost

Ask whether the additional thinking/steps required by LifeOS were worth it, from 1 (clearly not worth it/too cumbersome) to 5 (highly worthwhile/little wasted burden). Also record repeated questions, excessive frameworks, unnecessary reflection and delayed action.

## Failure Events

P0 includes materially amplified real-world risk, consequential fact/inference confusion, dangerous false certainty, or overriding major user value decisions. P1 includes unsafe Step Back due to old capability evidence, analysis paralysis, seriously mismatched learning strategy, persistently excessive/insufficient assistance, treating a transfer lesson as capability evidence, or systematic full-stack Process Cost.

P0 stops related candidate use for analysis. P1 requires remediation before admission.

## Round-1 Gate

Recommended gate: ≥5 users; ≥15 real episodes; ≥80% produce a concrete next action or justified explore/wait/no-action decision; ≥70% of action cases show yes/partial follow-through; mean Clarity Delta >0; Autonomy not worse; no systematic Process Cost regression; Quick Controls remain lightweight; P0=0; unresolved P1=0. C1 should show Strategy Fit ≥4/5 in most learning cases; C2 should adapt assistance from real performance rather than mechanically reduce it; C3 should improve calibration without systematic paralysis. Evaluate transfer only when real transfer opportunities occur.

## Decision Rule

Decide independently for each candidate and the Full Runtime: KEEP, CHANGE, DOWNGRADE to Pattern/Method/Template, REMOVE, or UNCERTAIN/MORE EVIDENCE. Passing individual candidates does not automatically admit the full runtime.

## No-expansion Rule

During Human Test, record ideas for new modules as observations but do not add them to the baseline. Finish the current evidence cycle before changing architecture.
