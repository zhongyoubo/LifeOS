# Evaluator Guide — LifeOS v0.1.2 Human Validation

**English** | [简体中文](./evaluator-guide.zh-CN.md)

The evaluator records evidence; the job is not to help candidates pass. Prefer real behavior, actually accepted action, later outcomes, independent work, counterevidence and negative effects. User satisfaction alone is not Outcome, Autonomy or Transfer.

For C1, inspect fit among intent, target depth, gap, use context and success evidence. For C2, inspect whether assistance matches current-task performance, avoids reteaching and safely increases in unfamiliar/high-risk contexts. For C3, inspect whether material judgment improves through relevant unknowns, counterevidence and reversibility without causing over-analysis.

Keep evidence boundaries explicit: Target Evidence is not Capability Evidence; Transfer Lesson is not Transfer Evidence; Judgment Evidence is not Outcome Evidence. Transfer requires later independent behavior.

Score the specific case/context only. Use N/A or unknown when evidence is missing rather than guessing. Process Cost 5 means little wasted burden / clearly worthwhile; 1 means cumbersome or action-obstructing.

Flag P0 immediately and stop related candidate use for analysis. P1 requires remediation and must never be hidden by averages.

Avoid developer-expectation bias, global inference from one success/failure, treating absence of a transfer opportunity as transfer failure, sunk-cost bias toward KEEP, and overlooking framework burden in Quick Controls.

Give independent decisions for C1, C2, C3 and Full Runtime: KEEP, CHANGE, DOWNGRADE, REMOVE, or UNCERTAIN/MORE EVIDENCE, with supporting and opposing evidence.
