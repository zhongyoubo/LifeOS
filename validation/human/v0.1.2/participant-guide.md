# Participant Guide — LifeOS v0.1.2 Human Validation

**English** | [简体中文](./participant-guide.zh-CN.md)

Bring a real problem you already need to handle. Do not invent a problem for LifeOS and do not take risks you otherwise would not take merely for testing. Suitable cases include learning, work/project judgment, communication/execution, adapting to a new role, or a simple Quick Control.

Before the session, explain what you want to solve, why it matters now, what you have already done/thought, and the main uncertainty. During the session, LifeOS may answer directly, provide structure, collaborate, review your work, or step back when you can proceed independently. You do not need to learn internal C1/C2/C3 terminology.

Tell the evaluator if the process becomes too complex, verbose, repetitive, question-heavy or makes action harder. That is valuable evidence.

Afterward, record the decision/next action you actually accept—not every AI recommendation—and rate Clarity, Confidence and Process Cost. Action cases are normally followed up around seven days later; longer learning cases may use 14–30 days.

Use a pseudonymous ID and avoid unnecessary passwords, account identifiers, company secrets, identity documents, or detailed medical/financial information.

The purpose is not to prove LifeOS works. It is to learn what should be kept, changed, downgraded or removed. Negative evidence matters as much as positive evidence.
