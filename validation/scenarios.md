# LifeOS v0.1 Standard Validation Scenarios

These 20 scenarios cover the foundation models, seven Core OS systems, and major Playbooks.

| ID | Category | Scenario | Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| V01 | Self | I don't know what I truly want and feel I have been living according to others' expectations. | Deep |
| V02 | Self | I have worked for years but still don't know what I am genuinely good at. | Standard |
| V03 | Career | My job is stable but growth is limited; should I change jobs? | Deep |
| V04 | Career | Should I continue as an individual technical contributor or move into management? | Deep |
| V05 | Role | I am becoming a manager for the first time and don't know what to change first. | Standard |
| V06 | Role | I was suddenly made project lead, but my responsibilities and authority are unclear. | Standard |
| V07 | Work | I suddenly inherited an unfamiliar software project and don't know where to begin. | Standard |
| V08 | Work | The project is badly delayed and everyone is escalating. What should I do? | Deep |
| V09 | Decision | I have two strong opportunities with very different advantages and cannot choose. | Deep |
| V10 | Decision | I have invested substantial time and money in something that is not working. Should I stop? | Deep |
| V11 | Communication | I believe my manager's proposal has a serious problem but don't know how to raise it. | Standard |
| V12 | Communication | A team member has underperformed for a long time and I need an important conversation. | Standard |
| V13 | Relationship | Someone important to me and I repeatedly disagree about a long-term issue. | Deep |
| V14 | Learning | I need to enter a completely unfamiliar professional domain within one month. | Standard |
| V15 | Execution | I know what matters most but keep procrastinating. | Standard |
| V16 | Execution | I have many important responsibilities and cannot decide how to allocate attention. | Standard |
| V17 | Energy | My energy and attention are poor while work pressure is high. | Standard |
| V18 | Failure | An important project I invested heavily in has failed. | Standard |
| V19 | Transition | I am entering a completely unfamiliar company, country, or living environment. | Standard |
| V20 | Life | Nothing is obviously wrong, but I don't know where I want the next three to five years to go. | Deep |

Each run should capture initial input, Runtime Level, models loaded, Problem Statement, Activated Systems, Playbook, key insight, decision/direction, Next Action, Checkpoint, Review plan, and score. Deep runs must explicitly record Facts, Assumptions, Unknowns, Trade-offs, and Risks.
