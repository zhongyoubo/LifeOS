# LifeOS Templates

**English** | [简体中文](./README.zh-CN.md)

Templates make LifeOS directly usable even without AI. They should remain short, copyable, and practical.

## 1. Problem Clarification

```text
Situation: What happened?
Problem: What is the real problem?
Impact: Why does it matter?
Facts: What is known?
Assumptions: What is being assumed?
Unknowns: What is still unknown?
Desired Outcome: What change is wanted?
Next Question: What is the most valuable question to answer next?
```

## 2. Decision

```text
Decision: What must be decided?
Why Now: Why now?
Goal: What outcome is desired?
Options: What real options exist?
Criteria: What matters in evaluating them?
Trade-offs: What is lost with each option?
Risks: What are the important risks?
Reversibility: Is the decision reversible?
Decision: What is chosen?
Reason: Why?
Revisit: Under what condition should it be reconsidered?
```
