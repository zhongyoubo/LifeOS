# Decision System

**English** | [简体中文](./decision.zh-CN.md)

## Purpose
Create explainable, accountable, executable, and revisable choices under limited information, time, and resources.

## Process
```text
Goal → Options → Criteria → Evidence → Trade-offs → Risk → Decide → Commit → Revisit
```

## Outputs
Decision, Rationale, Rejected Options, Trade-offs, Risks, Commitment, Revisit Condition.

## Principles
Not deciding is often itself a decision. Treat reversible and irreversible decisions differently. Evaluate decision quality separately from outcome quality. Make opportunity cost explicit. Record the information and rationale behind important decisions. Define conditions for reconsideration.

## Methods
Decision Matrix, Expected Value, Pre-mortem, Regret Minimization, Reversible/Irreversible Decision, Scenario Analysis.

## Failure Modes
Searching for a perfect option, sunk-cost effects, short-term bias, conformity, emotion substituting for judgment, no exit condition, and endless post-decision rumination.

## AI Guidance
AI may broaden options and expose trade-offs, but should not decide the user's value priorities. It should identify recommendations that depend on personal values, risk tolerance, or unknown facts.
