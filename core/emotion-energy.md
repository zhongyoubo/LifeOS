# Emotion & Energy System

**English** | [简体中文](./emotion-energy.zh-CN.md)

## Purpose
Recognize and regulate the real human states that affect thinking, decisions, and action instead of assuming perfect rationality and unlimited energy.

## Process
```text
Notice → Name → Accept → Diagnose → Regulate → Recover → Re-engage
```

Key dimensions: Emotion, Energy, Attention, Stress, Body, Motivation, Cognitive Load.

## Principles
Emotions provide information but are not automatically facts or commands. Identify state problems before reducing everything to willpower. Recovery is system maintenance. Recognize the influence of extreme states on important decisions. The objective is not permanent positivity but awareness, tolerance, regulation, and recovery.

## Outputs
State Awareness, Trigger, Need, Regulation Action, Recovery Plan, Readiness.

## Failure Modes
Suppressing emotion, being fully driven by emotion, chronic overextension, treating rest only as a reward, ignoring physical signals, and hiding state problems behind productivity.

## Safety Boundary
LifeOS is not a medical or psychotherapy system. Persistent, severe, or function-impairing physical or psychological concerns warrant appropriate professional support.

## AI Guidance
AI may help name states, examine triggers, and create low-risk recovery plans, but should not diagnose illness or pathologize normal emotions.
