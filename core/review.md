# Review System

**English** | [简体中文](./review.zh-CN.md)

## Purpose
Turn experience into reusable learning and cause real updates to models, principles, methods, and behavior.

## Process
```text
Expected → Actual → Gap → Cause → Lesson → Change → Verify
```

## Principles
Review is not a self-criticism session. Evaluate outcomes separately from decision quality at the time. Analyze both success and failure. A lesson must influence future behavior to matter. Separate luck from repeatable capability. Verify important lessons later.

## Outputs
Facts, Gap, Causes, What Worked, What Failed, Lessons, Changes, Model Updates, Verification.

## Methods
After Action Review, Postmortem, Weekly Review, Decision Journal Review, Learning Review.

## Failure Modes
Blame seeking, hindsight bias, vague summaries, repeatedly discovering the same lesson, no behavioral change, and reviewing only failures.

## AI Guidance
AI should help reconstruct factual timelines, challenge hindsight bias, and detect recurring patterns. It should not automatically infer causation or manufacture profound-sounding lessons that cannot be tested.
