# Contributing to LifeOS

LifeOS is intended to become a community-developed personal operating system rather than a collection of disconnected productivity tips.

## What We Welcome

Contributions may include:

- improvements to the core methodology;
- self, role, context, and goal models;
- thinking and decision frameworks;
- communication and execution practices;
- learning and reflection methods;
- practical LifeOS playbooks;
- templates and examples;
- AI-native workflows and skills;
- research and references that strengthen the framework.

## Contribution Principles

A contribution should preferably be:

1. **Human-centered** — respect individual values and autonomy.
2. **Practical** — lead toward clearer understanding or useful action.
3. **Reusable** — work across more than one narrow situation when possible.
4. **Transparent** — distinguish evidence, assumptions, opinions, and values.
5. **Adaptable** — avoid treating one personality, culture, career, or life path as universal.
6. **Reflective** — include feedback and learning where appropriate.
7. **Composable** — fit into the broader LifeOS architecture rather than becoming an isolated method.

## Playbook Contributions

New playbooks should explain:

- Situation
- Goal
- Context
- Diagnosis
- Questions to think through
- Options and trade-offs
- Communication needs
- Actions
- Checkpoints
- Review
- Learning

## Development Approach

LifeOS will evolve incrementally. During the early versions, architectural clarity is more important than the number of frameworks or playbooks.

Please use issues to discuss substantial changes before introducing major new concepts.
